What's actually in the teardown

This isn't a generic audit template. We go through your store the way a buyer — and Google — actually experiences it, and flag the specific things costing you traffic, conversions, or margin.

Search & technical SEO

Duplicate content between your /collections/.../products/ URLs and their canonical versions, thin or templated collection page content, missing or broken product schema, and page-speed drag from app or theme script bloat.

Conversion & product pages

Mobile product page friction, checkout steps that lose people, and product descriptions that describe the product but don't answer the buyer's actual question.

Analytics & product mix

Whether your GA4 setup can actually tell you which products are profitable at the margin level — not just which ones sell the most units.

Paid ad efficiency

Where ad spend is going toward traffic that was never going to convert, and where it's being pulled away from the products and audiences that actually work.
